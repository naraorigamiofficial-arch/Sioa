About VAGH

Our Philosophy

"Great origami is not about making more folds. It is about making the right ones." VAGH 2026 Competition Philosophy

VAGH was built on a simple belief: origami should stay both accessible and respected as an art form. Rather than rewarding complexity for its own sake, judging looked at precision, shaping, and overall execution, so a beautifully folded traditional model could stand alongside an advanced technical piece.

This was only the first edition. Thank you to every artist who folded, photographed, and shared their work. We hope to see even more of India's origami community at the next VAGH.

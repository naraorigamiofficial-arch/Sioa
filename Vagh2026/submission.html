Open to Everyone

Any person from any background may participate. There are no prerequisites or qualifications required.

Any Model

Fold any model you like — traditional, modular, wet-fold, or original. Complexity is not a judging criterion.

Judging Criteria

Entries are evaluated on cleanliness, precision, shaping quality, and overall presentation — not complexity.

Original Work Only

Submitted models must be folded by the participant. Entries using automated tools or pre-folded bases will be disqualified.

Constructive Feedback

Participants may opt in to receive personalised feedback from our panel of expert judges.

Awards & Recognition

Category winners receive physical trophies, national certificates, and are featured on the SIOA platform.

IMPORTANT

Submit a minimum of three clear photographs showing the model from multiple angles. Ensure good lighting and a clean background for best evaluation results.

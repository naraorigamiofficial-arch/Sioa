About

Society of Indian Origami Artists

Overview

The Society of Indian Origami Artists is an independent national institution committed to the advancement, documentation, and formal recognition of origami as both a fine art and a rigorous discipline within India.

SIOA approaches paper folding not as a pastime but as a structured practice at the intersection of mathematics, craftsmanship, and artistic expression — providing the infrastructure, platform, and community for practitioners at every stage of their journey.

Core Pillars
Vidhāna
विधान System & Execution
Establishing structural frameworks, clear rules, and high technical benchmarks to elevate the quality of physical execution across the community.
Abhyāsa
अभ्यास Disciplined Practice
Technical growth through consistent, deliberate practice. SIOA promotes the development of mathematical maturity, structural intuition, and the analytical study of complex folding systems.
Gaurava
गौरव Prestige & Integrity
Upholding authenticity, protecting creative ownership, and establishing a national standard of excellence that commands recognition both within India and on the international stage.
Hasta
हस्त Hand & Craft
Honouring the physical act of folding, the exactness of crease placement, the integrity of the paper surface, and the deliberate sculptural decisions that define refined, mature origami work.
Institutional Mandate
01
The Archive — VIDHĀNA Journal
The foundational academic and artistic record of the society. VIDHĀNA focuses on teaching origami design and overall knowledge of the art through curated publication and documentation.
02
The National Circuit — VAGH
Annual competitive cycles evaluated by a global consensus jury, structured to challenge and formally recognise technical and artistic talent at the national level.
03
Educational Infrastructure
Developing resources, workshops, and learning tracks that make rigorous origami education accessible to practitioners across skill levels and geographies.
04
Community & Recognition
Building a connected national network of artists, chapters, and institutions, creating opportunities for collaboration, exposure, and long-term career development in origami.
Philosophy
The ultimate beauty of a folded form does not rely on arbitrary complexity, but on the flawless convergence of precision, discipline, and structural truth.

Competition

National Origami Competition

VAGH 2026 is the inaugural national origami competition established by the Society of Indian Origami Artists. Designed as a foundational, long-term initiative rather than a singular event, it aims to cultivate a professional, artist-focused, and highly respected origami culture within India.

The competition shifts the paradigm away from pure technical complexity, prioritising instead the fundamental pillars of the Art, precision, execution, and artistic maturity. Participants are free to submit any model of their choice, and a beautifully folded simple work is evaluated with equal seriousness to an advanced technical piece.

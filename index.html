A little about us

There's a place
for you here.

The Society of Indian Origami Artists is a community for you to share what you know, learn from another folder, and find company in your practice.

Rooted in India and open beyond it, SIOA brings together artists, designers, teachers and enthusiasts. Whether you're making your first fold or developing your own designs, you can help shape what comes next.

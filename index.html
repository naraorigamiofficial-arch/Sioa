01 · About SIOA

One community.
Many ways to fold.

SIOA brings India's origami community into one shared space. We support meaningful exchange, responsible practice and stronger connections between people working across the art.

Why we exist

To give Indian origami a place to belong.

Artists learn from fellow artists, ideas travel across regions and every thoughtful contribution helps the community become more visible, connected and confident.

Connect

Find your people.

Discover folders, designers, educators and enthusiasts working throughout India.

Learn

Exchange knowledge.

Share methods, experiences and perspectives that strengthen everyone's practice.

Participate

Take part.

Join competitions, community projects and new opportunities as SIOA grows.

Contribute

Shape what comes next.

SIOA is built by its members and made stronger by their ideas and initiative.

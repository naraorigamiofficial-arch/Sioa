01 · About SIOA

You don't have to fold alone.

Maybe you design. Maybe you teach. Maybe you simply love the moment a sheet becomes something else. Here, you can meet people who understand that feeling.

Why this place exists

Your work has somewhere to go.

From your first fold to the piece you've spent years refining, your practice deserves conversation, care and a place in India's origami story.

Connect

Meet someone who gets it.

Find folders, artists, designers and teachers across India.

Learn

Ask. Try. Share.

Participate

Take part in competitions and community projects as they happen.

Contribute

Make this yours.

Your ideas can help decide what SIOA becomes next.
